# Autonomous Internet Revenue Engine — Mission Constitution

**Mission start:** 2026-11-09  
**Target window:** 10–30 days  
**Target:** ₹10,00,00,000 (₹10 crore) in actual, cleared, received revenue  
**Starting capital:** ₹0  
**Operating principle:** maximum feasible AI, internet, and free automation; human involvement only for identity, KYC, account authorization, legal approvals, and other non-delegable actions.

## 1. Mission definition

Build and operate a lawful, evidence-led internet revenue system from the current Day Zero state. The system may combine multiple compatible revenue channels, including digital products, software/tools, productized services, licensing, affiliate/referral distribution, reseller/partner distribution, content-led acquisition, and other validated models.

No revenue model is pre-selected. Selection must follow demand, willingness-to-pay, time-to-first-payment, distribution feasibility, payment eligibility in India, delivery automation, and unit-economics checks.

## 2. Hard constraints

- No paid APIs, paid hosting, paid ads, paid subscriptions, or required upfront capital at launch.
- Prefer open-source software, free tiers, public data, and platforms with verifiable free access.
- Do not assume an existing audience, product, customer base, mailing list, or traffic.
- Do not reuse unrelated historic business concepts as the default strategy.
- The existing `ai-content-system` repository may be reused only after technical, security, policy, and commercial-use audit.
- No fabricated testimonials, fake orders, fake views, engagement manipulation, spam, deceptive claims, scraped personal-data abuse, or copyright infringement.
- Do not publish low-value mass-generated content. Every asset must have a clear audience, original utility, and a measurable distribution purpose.
- Never commit secrets, access tokens, OAuth refresh tokens, private customer data, or personal identifiers to this public repository.

## 3. Revenue accounting

Track separately:
1. Gross order value
2. Platform/gateway fees
3. Refunds, chargebacks, cancellations
4. Taxes/withholding where applicable
5. Net settled amount
6. Bank/eligible payment account settlement date and reference

**Mission KPI:** cleared funds actually received and reconciled. Views, followers, impressions, clicks, leads, signups, unpaid invoices, pending payouts, and crypto wallet valuations are not revenue.

## 4. Operating architecture

- Opportunity Intelligence: research markets, customer pain, demand signals, competitor offers, pricing, and proof.
- Product/Offer Factory: create the smallest useful deliverable with clear scope and verifiable value.
- Content & Distribution Engine: create original, platform-compliant content and distribute it across permitted channels.
- Sales & Partner Engine: self-serve checkout, compliant outreach, affiliate/reseller onboarding, and follow-up.
- Delivery & Support Engine: automated fulfilment, documentation, onboarding, and customer support.
- Revenue Ledger: orders, fees, refunds, settlements, and reconciliation.
- Governance & Evidence: source links, test results, approvals, change log, and audit trail.

## 5. Execution gates

A component moves through: `planned → built → tested → deployed → verified`.

A successful code run does not prove a sale. A successful upload does not prove monetization. A successful checkout does not prove settlement. Every claim must be supported by the corresponding evidence.

## 6. Human-in-the-loop boundary

The operator may be asked only for actions that require their own identity, credentials, legal consent, KYC, payment-account authorization, publishing permissions, or explicit approval of a consequential external action. Never ask the operator to paste secrets into chat or commit them to GitHub. Use provider secret managers and official OAuth flows.

## 7. Target integrity

₹10 crore within 10–30 days is an extreme stretch target, not a guaranteed or forecastable outcome from a zero-asset starting point. The system must pursue the highest legitimate upside while reporting base, stretch, and downside scenarios honestly. Do not change accounting definitions to make the target appear achieved.
