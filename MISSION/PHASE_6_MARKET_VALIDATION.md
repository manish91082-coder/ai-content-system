# Phase 6 — Market Evidence & Validation Plan

## Chosen pilot offer
**Admission Desk Lite:** a Hindi-first, offline enquiry tracker plus follow-up message templates and a daily desk SOP for small coaching institutes and tuition centres.

## Evidence reviewed (9 Nov 2026 research snapshot)
1. India SME Forum's 2025 DigiShastra report says its survey covered 7,835 MSMEs during 2024–25; 53.8% had adopted at least one digital tool, and 52.6% of respondents found identifying suitable digital tools somewhat or very difficult. This supports a tool-discovery/usability problem, not demand for this specific product.
   - https://indiasmeforum.org/digishaastra/assets/docs/Final-META-Report-Card-2025.pdf
2. A July 2026 report on the Google + India SME Forum + Deloitte study says 75% of surveyed MSMEs faced skill gaps and 60% cited budget limitations. It also reports 34% use AI for content generation. These are broad MSME findings and do not establish coaching-centre purchase intent.
   - https://www.fortuneindia.com/amp/story/business-news/msme-digital-advertising-generated-49731-crore-in-economic-output-in-2025-google-report/147073
3. Indian vendor listings show coaching/education CRM products at advertised starting prices around ₹599/month and ₹1,499/month. This confirms existing competition and a software category, not the actual number of customers or verified transactions.
   - https://appadvisor.in/whatsapp-crm-for-education
   - https://klozent.ai/whatsapp-crm-for-coaching-institutes.html
4. Published Indian social-media service packages range widely, but the product deliberately avoids competing as a generic content-posting subscription.

## Critical evidence limits
- The market reports are surveys with differing samples, definitions and sponsors. They cannot be combined into one adoption rate.
- Vendor price pages are seller claims, not audited sales or customer conversion data.
- No verified willingness-to-pay evidence for Admission Desk Lite exists yet.
- No coaching-centre interviews, paid pilots, customer lists, traffic or distribution channel have been confirmed.
- This is a hypothesis-led pilot. It must not be described as a proven product-market fit.

## Why this offer
- It solves a narrow operational task: keeping enquiry stages and next follow-up dates visible.
- It is a tangible downloadable tool, not a generic AI content generator.
- Offline/local storage reduces hosting and recurring infrastructure requirements.
- It can be demonstrated immediately and delivered as a file.
- It does not require WhatsApp API approval or paid API calls.

## Proposed validation experiment
1. Prepare a public demo using fictional data only.
2. Contact a small, manually selected set of coaching-centre owners through legitimate public business channels. No bulk scraping, unsolicited mass messaging or automated spam.
3. Ask about their current enquiry process, follow-up failures, staff workflow, and whether they would pay for a local/offline tool.
4. Show the demo only after interest.
5. Offer a transparent one-time pilot price only after interviews. Test a price range rather than claiming it is market-established.
6. Count a sale only after payment is captured and later reconciled to the bank statement.
7. If no qualified buyer shows meaningful intent, revise or reject the product rather than inflating the market case.

## Decision gates
- **Continue:** at least 3 independent owners describe the same problem and at least 1 makes a real paid pilot purchase.
- **Revise:** problem is real but users need a different workflow or price.
- **Stop:** no credible problem evidence after 10 qualified conversations, or users consistently prefer existing free/low-cost tools.

These are internal experiment thresholds, not external market benchmarks.
