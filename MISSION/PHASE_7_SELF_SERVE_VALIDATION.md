# Phase 7 — Self-Serve Demo & Customer Validation

## Deliverables
- `product/landing.html`: truthful product landing page with demo and app links.
- `product/demo.html`: interactive fictional enquiry dataset; search, stage filter, stage changes and simulated metrics.
- Both pages are static, dependency-free and do not collect or transmit personal data.

## Positioning
Admission Desk Lite is an offline-first enquiry and follow-up desk for small coaching centres and tuition institutes. It is not positioned as a full CRM, WhatsApp automation platform, or AI product.

## Pricing experiment (not a price decision)
Test two one-time pilot hypotheses: ₹299 and ₹499. Ask qualified owners what they would pay after observing their current workflow and trying the demo. These are experiment points only, not validated prices, offers already sold, or market benchmarks. No checkout is active.

## Validation protocol
1. Select a small number of independent coaching-centre owners through public, legitimate business channels.
2. Ask about enquiry volume, current register/software, missed follow-ups, staff handoffs, and CSV comfort before showing the demo.
3. Observe a real task using fictional or owner-provided non-sensitive sample data. Do not collect student records.
4. Record objections and requested workflow changes with permission. Do not record personal contact details in this repository.
5. Ask whether the owner would pay ₹299 or ₹499 once for the pilot and why. A stated preference is not a sale.
6. Count a paid pilot only after an actual captured payment; count settled revenue only after bank reconciliation.

## Decision gates
- Continue: 3 independent owners confirm the same recurring problem and at least 1 completes a genuine paid pilot.
- Revise: problem confirmed but required workflow/price differs.
- Stop: no credible recurring problem after 10 qualified conversations, or users prefer existing free/low-cost tools.

## Operational limits
- No backend, lead capture, telemetry, payment gateway, checkout, cloud sync, or automated fulfilment is included in Phase 7.
- Do not publish or advertise until an owner-approved public hosting/domain and contact route exist.
- Do not claim customer count, testimonials, conversion, product-market fit, or revenue without evidence.
- No unsolicited bulk messaging, scraping, fake urgency, fake reviews, or fabricated outcomes.
- Payment/KYC, legal/tax review and live payment authorization remain owner-controlled gates.

## Next gate
Run a real, small usability and willingness-to-pay test. Only after evidence exists should Phase 8 implement an owner-approved contact capture, checkout and fulfilment path.
