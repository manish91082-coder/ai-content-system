## Sampling and permission-first opening
Start with three independent centres representing different workflows. Ask for the owner or person responsible for enquiry follow-up. Do not infer institute size from review count.

“नमस्ते, हम छोटे coaching centres के enquiry और follow-up workflow पर एक छोटा product research कर रहे हैं। अभी कुछ बेचने या payment लेने नहीं आए हैं। क्या आप 8–10 मिनट दे सकते हैं ताकि समझ सकें कि enquiries अभी कैसे दर्ज होती हैं और follow-up कैसे manage होता है? अगर अभी समय ठीक नहीं है तो हम यहीं रुकेंगे।”

## Stop rules
- If the person declines, thank them and stop. Do not repeat-call or add them to a marketing list.
- No bulk WhatsApp messaging or scraping personal contact details.
- Do not record names, phone numbers, student records or confidential business figures in the worksheet.
- A verbal price preference is not a sale.
