## 4. ANTI-DRIFT CONSTITUTION

Every task must map to at least one of:
- Discover a credible path to paid demand.
- Create or acquire a sellable asset needed for that path.
- Publish/list/distribute it through an authorized channel.
- Acquire customers without manual outreach.
- Convert self-serve demand into captured payment.
- Fulfil and support automatically.
- Reconcile cleared bank settlement.
- Improve conversion, margin, reliability, or time-to-cash.

Before executing a task, record:
`TASK → REVENUE HYPOTHESIS → EVIDENCE → EXECUTION → TEST → MEASURED RESULT → NEXT DECISION`

Reject or defer work with no material connection to the 30-day bank-settlement objective.

### Required operating rules
- Revenue-first, not code-first.
- Time-to-settlement matters as much as theoretical market size.
- Validate platform eligibility, payout country, KYC, payout minimums, reserves/holds, settlement schedules, fees, refund exposure, and restrictions before relying on a channel.
- Prefer channels with existing demand/discovery and short payout cycles over building an audience from zero, unless evidence says otherwise.
- Do not depend on paid advertising when capital is zero.
- Do not violate platform rules, spam, scrape unlawfully, fake reviews/orders/traffic, impersonate people, manipulate rankings, infringe copyright, or misuse personal data.
- No deceptive claims, fake scarcity, fabricated testimonials, or unsubstantiated earnings promises.
- Do not use customer funds for reinvestment until cleared and legally available.
- Preserve audit trails, source provenance, transaction IDs, and reproducible test evidence.
- Do not merge stacked PRs or change protected production settings without explicit user authorization where required.
